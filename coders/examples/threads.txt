
         PTHREAD CHEATSHEET


pthread_create      →  launch a thread
pthread_join        →  wait for a thread to finish

pthread_mutex_init      →  "here is a new token, it's free"
pthread_mutex_lock      →  "I'm taking the token"
pthread_mutex_unlock    →  "I'm giving the token back"
pthread_mutex_destroy   →  "we're done, get rid of the token"

pthread_cond_init       →  "here is a new signal flag, it's idle"
pthread_cond_wait       →  "I'm sleeping until someone signals me"
pthread_cond_broadcast  →  "wake up everyone waiting"
pthread_cond_signal     →  "wake up one person waiting"
pthread_cond_destroy    →  "we're done, get rid of the signal flag"

gettimeofday        →  get current time
usleep(n)           →  sleep for n microseconds

=====================================
mutex  →  controls ACCESS ("only one at a time")
cond   →  signals something HAPPENED ("hey wake up")
